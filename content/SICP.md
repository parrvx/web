# Building Abstractions with Procedures

We are about to study the idea of computational process. Computational processes are abstract beings that inhabit computers... In effect, we conjure the spirits of the computer with our spell.

Computational process is indeed much like a sorcerer's ideia of spirit. The programs we use to conjure processes are like a sorcerer's spells composed of arcane called programing languages.

**Programing in Lisp**

[Lisp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisp_(programming_language)) was invented in the late 1950's and is an acronym for LISt Processing, was designed to provide symbol-manipulating capabilities for attacking programing problems such as the symbolic differentiation and integration of algebric expressions. Among it's features are, the fact Lisp descriptions processes, called *procedures*, can themselves be represented and manipulated as Lisp data. This allow's us to study techniques of program-design that rely on the ability to blur the tradicional distinction between "passive" data and "active" processes, and writting programs that must manipulate other programs as data, such as *interpreters* and *compilers* that support computer languages.
